Cotswolds Windrush extract, cropped for the bundled example.

Elevation
  AWS Open Data Terrain Tiles, Terrarium PNG, zoom 14, tiles X 8110-8111
  and Y 5417-5418, cropped to 51.905 N-51.920 N and 1.790 W-1.768 W.
  The tiles are a Tilezen composite (NASA SRTM plus regional models; over
  Europe that is commonly EU-DEM). They are not a raw Copernicus GLO-30 file.
  https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
  https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md

Land cover and waterways

They are not a redistribution of the full datasets.
